## Appointment = 8 tabs (consultation protocol, free navigation between tabs)
1 Information: reason/goals, habits, usual intake, diet & disease history; dated free-text observations kept across appointments; attach files (questionnaires, labs, old plans); set goals; food-diary entry box top-right.
2 Follow-up: calendar of client app logs; color per day: Green = no significant changes to plan, Orange = some, Red = many (added/removed foods, qty changes, added/removed meals), Grey = not enough info; water bar vs goal (blue); exercise icon (green); activity feed + photos/messages column; chat at bottom.
3 Measurements: 38 anthropometric, 63 blood parameters, 31 body-composition fields (choose visible ones); "register multiple at once"; skinfold → %fat via several predictive equations; InBody integration; evolution graph per measurement; PDF report; goals per measurement with target date.
4 Planning: shows weight, %fat, BMI category; goal weight & fat-mass goal or recommended (J.D. Robinson); WHO growth charts for children/teens; PAL by 4 coefficients or by individual activities (METs); BMR from 10 predictive equations; total energy needs; up to 7 plannings (e.g., training/rest days), renameable; macro + fiber distribution by typing % or sliding bars; shows grams and g/kg; choose nutrient recommendation source.
5 Meals: 15+ food composition databases; up to 7 meal-plan versions (+ icon) = weekly plan, each linked to a planning; default meals breakfast, morning snack, lunch, afternoon snack, dinner, supper (rename, time, delete, add); live kcal/macro calc; right-side column with client needs + latest food diary; add food by search, sort by nutrient, filter by database / own foods / food groups / equivalents / own recipes; per-food edit serving, rename, view nutrients, delete (slide); templates (built-in + own); import template; SCHEDULED imports (e.g., new plan each week for a month); import single meals or from other clients; save as template or as client file; PDF.
6 Recommendations: physical activity from exercise DB with METs → kcal/day & weekly breakdown; foods to avoid (templates); water goal in 0.5 L steps; free-text other recommendations; all templatable; client reports compliance in app.
7 Analysis: planned vs current; macro distribution per meal; water distribution; micronutrient requirements met? (3 recommendation sources, age/sex adapted); top contributing foods per nutrient; per-day plan analysis; PDF.
8 Deliverables: app access (email instructions or random password); email PDF or print; per-appointment dated notes; per-client app permissions (app access, messages, food diary, weight logs, appointment confirmation); finish appointment (flag icon).
Vertical menu: Homepage (today's tasks), Calendar, Clients, Messages, Follow-up, Foods, Recipes, Equivalents, Templates, Activities; Profile; personal website + blog; privacy/notifications; settings; referral program. Also: online booking + booking requests, telehealth integration, payments/invoicing, admin/staff access, appointment reminders, branding logo on PDFs, HIPAA/GDPR, AI summaries (current site).
Visual: brand green gradient (#3ee07c → #12a58a approx. from video cover), white rounded logo; marketing site light theme. No in-app UI screenshots could be retrieved.

## Visual observations (from Nutrium's own published product screenshots on nutrium.com/professionals; crops in docs/team/nutrium_ui/)
- Desktop web app, light: white cards on very light grey, thin borders, rounded 8–12px, generous whitespace; narrow icon-only left vertical rail (icons ~18px) with brand heart logo on top; top bar: page title + bell/mail/card icons + avatar/name.
- Appointment page: client header card (photo, name, tag chips "Weight loss / New client / VIP Client", birth date, phone, edit/mail/calendar icons); horizontal tab row: Information · Measurements · Planning · Meal plan · Progress · Recommendations · Deliverables; orange "End appointment" button top-right.
- Meal plan tab: plan selector "Everyday" (versions per day group) + toolbar icons + segmented toggle "Creation | Analysis". Each meal = card: time "07:30 AM" + name "Breakfast", collapse/menu; food rows as text "200 grams of chicken, broiler or fryers, breast" with drag handle, swap & delete icons; alternative foods shown inline as "or" rows under a food; "Add new food +" dashed row; Notes field; meal footer = small donut + chips Energy/Protein/Carbs/Fat/Fiber with values.
- Right sticky summary card: Energy 150/1920 kcal bar (purple) + donut + per-macro bars "Protein 19g/60g", "Carbohydrates 124g/200g", "Fat 5g/40g (↑22)", "Fiber 2g/20g"; below: per-meal mini donuts (Breakfast/Lunch/Snack).
- "Add equivalents" panel: search + database filter; "Calculate equivalents according to reference food": table rows with amount (e.g., 36 g / household measure "Half spoon") computed so macros match the reference food; per-macro difference chips; "+" to add.
- Planning summary table: Protein / Carbohydrate / Fat rows → kcal, %, g, g/kg.
- "Create new meal plan version": choose days (Mon…Sun chips) + "copy meals of meal plan" dropdown.
- Macro color code + icons (consistent everywhere): Energy purple (flame), Protein blue ~#78a8f0 (diamond), Carbs orange ~#d89060 (circle), Fat yellow ~#f0c048 (atom), Fiber teal ~#60c0a8 (triangle). Brand: mint/teal (#3ee07c→#12a58a gradient; heart logo).
- Client app: "What were your meals like?" per meal with Skipped / Changed / As planned; hydration; push "Well done! Your dinner got a like!"; weight chart week/month/year; next appointment card with Confirm/Manage/Join call; recipe cards with photo, times, ingredients; educational infographics.
